# Pacifica

Pacifica is a bank building a mobile app to bring Gen Z into banking.

Younger customers expect banking to feel as simple and social as the apps they already use, so Pacifica aims to be a modern, mobile-first bank that earns their trust and their everyday spending.

## Design

a clean, minimal interface with a prominent cash balance, simple actions such as adding money and withdrawing, and easy-to-scan cards.
